Avocado Egg Toast

Creamy avocado on toasted sourdough, topped with jammy boiled eggs, herbs, and cracked pepper. A five-ingredient breakfast that still feels complete.

12 min 1 serving Vegetarian High protein
Start Cooking

Ingredients

  • Sourdough slice 1
  • Ripe avocado ½
  • Eggs 2
  • Lemon juice 1 tsp
  • Chili flakes pinch
  • Fresh herbs to garnish
  • Olive oil 1 tsp
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil eggs for 8 minutes, then cool and peel.
  2. Toast the sourdough and mash avocado with lemon, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread avocado on the toast and layer sliced eggs on top.
  4. Finish with olive oil, chili flakes, and herbs. Eat while the toast is warm.
