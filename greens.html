Crunchy Garden Salad

A crisp mix of greens, shredded carrot, olives, and toasted walnuts. Simple ingredients, lots of texture, and a bright vinaigrette to pull it together.

15 min 4 servings Vegan Gluten free
Start Cooking

Ingredients

  • Mixed salad greens 4 cups
  • Carrot, shredded 1
  • Kalamata olives ⅓ cup
  • Toasted walnuts ⅓ cup
  • Red onion, thin slices ¼
  • Apple cider vinegar 1 tbsp
  • Extra-virgin olive oil 3 tbsp
  • Dijon mustard 1 tsp

Steps

  1. Whisk olive oil, vinegar, Dijon, salt, and pepper into a vinaigrette.
  2. Add greens, carrot, onion, and olives to a wide bowl.
  3. Toss with dressing just before serving so the leaves stay crisp.
  4. Scatter toasted walnuts over the top and serve immediately.
