Pesto Farfalle Salad

Tender bowtie pasta tossed in basil pesto and served with a simple green salad. Light enough for lunch, satisfying enough to share at the table.

20 min 3 servings Vegetarian Meal prep
Start Cooking

Ingredients

  • Farfalle pasta 250 g
  • Basil pesto ⅓ cup
  • Baby arugula 2 cups
  • Lemon zest 1 tsp
  • Parmesan, shaved ¼ cup
  • Olive oil 1 tbsp
  • Toasted pine nuts 2 tbsp
  • Salt & black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil farfalle in salted water until al dente, then drain and cool slightly.
  2. Toss the pasta with pesto, olive oil, lemon zest, and a pinch of pepper.
  3. Fold in arugula so the leaves just wilt from the warmth of the pasta.
  4. Top with parmesan and pine nuts. Serve warm or chilled.
