Pan-Seared Salmon with Herb Salsa

Crisp-skinned salmon served over wilted greens and finished with a bright cucumber herb salsa. A protein-rich dinner that feels special without extra effort.

30 min 2 servings High protein Gluten free
Start Cooking

Ingredients

  • Salmon fillets 2
  • Olive oil 2 tbsp
  • Cucumber, diced ½
  • Fresh parsley & cilantro ¼ cup
  • Lime juice 1 tbsp
  • Baby greens 3 cups
  • Garlic clove 1
  • Sea salt & pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Pat salmon dry, season with salt and pepper, then sear skin-side down in olive oil until crisp.
  2. Flip and cook until just opaque in the center. Rest for two minutes.
  3. Toss cucumber, herbs, lime, and minced garlic into a quick salsa.
  4. Serve the salmon over warm greens and spoon salsa over the top.
