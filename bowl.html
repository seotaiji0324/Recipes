Rainbow Avocado Power Bowl

A colorful plant-based bowl layered with creamy avocado, roasted sweet potato, chickpeas, and crisp vegetables. Balanced, filling, and ready in under half an hour.

25 min 2 servings Vegan High fiber
Start Cooking

Ingredients

  • Ripe avocado 1
  • Cooked chickpeas 1 cup
  • Roasted sweet potato 1 cup
  • Cherry tomatoes ½ cup
  • Red cabbage, shredded ½ cup
  • Watermelon radish 4 slices
  • Mixed greens 2 cups
  • Lemon olive oil dressing 3 tbsp

Steps

  1. Roast cubed sweet potato at 200°C until tender and lightly browned.
  2. Arrange greens in two bowls, then add chickpeas, sweet potato, cabbage, tomato, and radish.
  3. Fan sliced avocado over the top and drizzle with lemon olive oil dressing.
  4. Finish with a pinch of salt and fresh herbs. Serve immediately.
